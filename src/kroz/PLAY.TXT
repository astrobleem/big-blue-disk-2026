KROZ: GRENADES -- ORIGINAL THREE-LEVEL SLICE

Explore three small dungeons. Collect + gems, k keys and w whip pickups;
open D doors and descend at >>. No mandatory treasure tally or Staff quest.
Monsters, terrain and route choices remain the focus. This is original code
and maps inspired by Kroz, with additive grenades; not an exact recreation.

ARROWS or NUMPAD 1-4,6-9: move (including diagonals).
I/J/K/M move up/left/right/down; U/O/comma supply NW/NE/SE alternatives.
W: nearby whip. SPACE: grenade. R: free checkpoint retry. N: skip a level.
ESC: return to the magazine. At the final descent, R explores again.
W is never up. N is skip rather than the old PCjr southwest alternative.

WHIP: one charge sweeps the eight adjacent tiles, clears nearby monsters,
rubble and forest, and extinguishes burning forest. Permanent walls, water,
doors and shielded diagonal corners stay intact. A brief white ~~ sweep shows
the action. Start with20 charges; each w pickup gives5. Holding W can repeat
after a short cooldown; queued repeats cannot spend multiple charges at once.
The radius, cost and guaranteed terrain break are documented new choices;
the supplied historical instructions do not establish their exact values.

GEMS: start with40. Each + pickup gives5. A monster disappears on contact
and takes up to2 gems. If already at0, contact gives a free checkpoint retry.
These generous values and automatic retry differ from historical game-over.
R restores this level's entry supplies and map; no lives limit. New levels
carry inventory, topping gems/whips up to40/20 if needed. No full save/load yet.

KEYS: stepping onto D spends one key. Neither weapon breaks locked doors.
Every authored level has a reachable key and route to its descending stairs.

GRENADES: unlimited, up to4 active; aim with movement. Travel up to4 tiles,
then explode after12 simulation steps. Barrels [] chain; rubble breaks;
forest burns briefly. Blasts cannot hurt you or destroy loot, keys, whip
pickups, portals or stairs, and cannot break permanent walls/water/doors.
Barrels push cardinally but cannot cover a pickup or portal.

WORLD: ~ rivers block travel; use authored crossings. Purple O pads pair
within a level. Step off the arrival pad before using it again. Fire ^ blocks
burning forest briefly, then leaves floor. It spreads only through forest,
with at most2 propagation generations; water/floor/walls stop it. Whip it
from nearby or wait. Loot and authored floor routes never burn. No fluid or
fortress simulation, fire contact damage, lava, pits or hidden lethal traps.

LEVELS: Entry Dungeon (8 slow monsters), River Passage (10), Ember Caverns
(12). Grenade clusters give crowd/terrain opportunities, not extra mandatory
combat gates. The last stairs finish this short original descent.

LIMITS: this is a small faithful-style subset, not the historic30-level game.
Manual keyboard feel and physical EX timing/memory still need acceptance.
The reference audit and exact source/qualification live in the companion ZIP.

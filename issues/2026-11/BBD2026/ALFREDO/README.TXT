ALFREDO: AFTER HOURS - EPISODE 2 - PROPOSED FOR THE NOVEMBER ISSUE
Not on a roster yet. October's episode and payload are unchanged.

4:00 AM. DJ Alfredo plays the last set at the Radio Shack Rave, clocks
out, and walks past the MOS Labs data center to his favorite fishing
spot: the plant's warm-water OUTFLOW. The pond is a live Flow-88 fluid
simulation. Then the training run finishes and the plant flushes its heat.

Needs a Tandy 1000 (320x200 16-color mode, SN76496 sound), plain DOS,
about 150K free memory. Runs about 2 minutes 45 seconds.

KEYS  Space/P pause   Left/Right scene   R replay   M mute   Esc exit
After the credits: F stays at the pond. Arrows move the lure, Space
stirs the water, H turns the heat flush on/off, C calms it, Esc back.

No resident code; the timer is only read, never reprogrammed. Video
mode, sound (silenced) and Ctrl-Break handling are restored on exit.
Emulator-tested only (DOSBox-X Tandy): smooth at 3000 cycles, about
4 to 10 pictures a second at 450. No physical Tandy timing or listening
is claimed. Story, pictures, music: Claude. Pond solver: Flow-88.

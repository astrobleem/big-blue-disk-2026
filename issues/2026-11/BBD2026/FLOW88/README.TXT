FLOW-88 - SEPARATE EXPERIMENTAL CANDIDATE, NOT ON AN ISSUE ROSTER
Plain DOS, compatible Tandy, existing80-column BIOS text mode3 only.
Arrows move; Space toggles injection; B obstacle; V vectors; O presets;
C clear; Q/Escape/Ctrl-C exit. Audio is omitted to preserve caller sound.
No mode, timer, speaker, PIT or PSG writes. Cursor/text/hook restored.
16-bit toy fluid solver; all seven density characters reachable.
Physical8088 speed unmeasured; the solver may be slow.
Source: src/flow88; exact candidate manifest in docs/candidates.
GPLv3. Gemini design, Claude port, Codex safety correction, submitted Chad.

YOUR TANDY HAS ANOTHER VOICE

Read ARTICLE.TXT. Use VOICE 1, VOICE 2, or VOICE 3.
VOICE alone runs all three brief original demonstrations.
1 Extra speaker melody / 2 Noise percussion / 3 Envelope and vibrato.
Escape or Ctrl-C stops. Plain DOS on a known-compatible Tandy only.
No driver installation; do not run competing sound producers.

VOICE.EXE was built MSC6/G0/AS;865 host checks and five native emulator
cases passed. Binary SHA256 is recorded in MANIFEST.JSN.
Source and BUILD.BAT require an existing licensed MSC6 toolchain.
This disk includes no compiler, OS, private music or test recordings.

REFS.TXT identifies the primary manuals. EX routing is independently
verified from the1986 Tandy manual, printed32; it is not assumed for
other models. No physical EX listening test is claimed.
Full QA recordings and evidence remain outside this disk directory.

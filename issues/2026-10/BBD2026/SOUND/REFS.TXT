Primary references and evidence scope

1. Tandy Corporation, Tandy 1000 EX Technical Reference Manual, 1986,
   Cat. No.25-1510; Main Logic Board, printed p.32, "Sound".
   Primary manufacturer's manual, archived at:
   https://archive.org/details/tandy1000extechnicalreferencemanual
   Read the item's FULL TEXT; the Sound paragraph immediately precedes
   printed32 and Figure10 on printed33. It describes four generators
   summed with the external8253 counter2 input; that input has
   programmable frequency and fixed amplitude. Figure11 is printed34.
   Independently verified through the archive's primary-manual OCR on
   2026-10-07. No local manual download or copied manual pages included.
   Audio Out selection uses SNDCNTL0/1; the article does not promise
   identical speaker/jack/mute routing across other Tandy models.

2. Texas Instruments, SN76489AN Digital Complex Sound Generator manual.
   Primary manufacturer's manual, mirrored at:
   https://map.grauw.nl/resources/sound/texas_instruments_sn76489an.pdf
   Web access resolves the nine-page PDF; no local download was made.
   Covers PSG tone/noise register and independent attenuation behavior.
   The Tandy's integrated implementation is not asserted to be a
   discrete SN76489AN. Do not import every variant's noise feedback or
   undocumented zero-divider behavior into the Tandy description.

3. Existing shared adapter, unchanged source copied into this folder:
   DOSSND.C, DOSSND.H, DOSGUARD.H, win30/DRIVER/PITCORE.H.
   Base source commit: 61d6c3cb5580d5f1c31a2950fc9eb67ef4d846fa.
   BIOS/session and speaker-busy refusal, cooperative resource checking,
   PIT note range45..96, timer2-only programming and port61 bit retention
   are implementation facts. They are not protection from arbitrary
   hardware writes by outside code, nor physical-machine certification.
